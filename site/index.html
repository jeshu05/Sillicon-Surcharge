Monitoring Hyperscale AI Expansion & Household Electricity Burdens

Silicon Surcharge is an independent empirical intelligence observatory that monitors where rapid hyperscale AI data center buildouts geographically coincide with rising residential electricity tariffs and community economic vulnerability across 60+ countries and major metropolitan energy corridors.

SCIENTIFIC FRAMEWORK & TRANSPARENCY

Silicon Surcharge Methodology & Non-Causal Analytics

1. Purpose and Analytical Scope

Silicon Surcharge is an evidence-surfacing research tool designed to monitor where rapid AI and hyperscale infrastructure expansion geographically coincides with residential electricity rate increases and household economic vulnerability.

Critical Analytical Principle: Silicon Surcharge detects spatial and temporal association. It does NOT claim or establish that data center construction directly causes residential utility rate increases. Utility rates are governed by regulated public utility commissions based on multifaceted capital investments, fuel market volatility, transmission upgrades, and statutory rate structures.

2. Mathematical Formulas

Rate Pressure Ratio ($RP$):

RP = %Δ Residential Electricity Rate (12-Month) / max(0.1, %Δ Median Household Income (12-Month))

AI Load Pressure ($AIP$):

AIP = min(100, (Planned MW × 1.0 + Construction MW × 0.85 + Operational MW × 0.4) / Baseline Threshold)

Power Pressure Score ($PPS$):

PPS = 0.40 × Norm(RP) + 0.35 × Norm(AIP) + 0.25 × Norm(Burden Delta)

3. Data Sources & Granularity

Data is acquired via Bright Data Scraper Studio across three primary public web domains:

  • AI Infrastructure: aidatacenterindex.com & public project listings (346+ facilities across 64 countries).
  • Utility Electricity Tariffs: globalpetrolprices.com & electricchoice.com (national and US state retail prices).
  • Community Economics: numbeo.com cost-of-living index & US Census Bureau income benchmarks.
